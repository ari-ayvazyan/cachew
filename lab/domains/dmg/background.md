# H3K27M diffuse midline glioma: background for the hypotheses

Each hypothesis in `model.py` cites a line range of this file. These are
claims from the literature, not results of this study.

## p53 axis
TP53 is the most frequent partner mutation of H3.3 K27M (Khuong-Quang 2012, Mackay 2017).
PPM1D truncating mutations occur in TP53-wild-type DIPG and inactivate p53 signalling (Zhang 2014).
No approved drug restores mutant p53; MDM2 inhibitors only apply to TP53-wild-type tumours.

## ACVR1 axis
ACVR1 activating mutations occur in about a quarter of DIPG, mostly with H3.1 K27M (Taylor 2014, Buczkowicz 2014).
ACVR1/ALK2 inhibitors have shown preclinical activity (Carvalho 2019).

## PI3K axis
PIK3CA, PIK3R1 and PTEN alterations are frequent in DMG and co-occur with ACVR1 in H3.1 (Mackay 2017).
Paxalisib (brain-penetrant PI3K/mTOR inhibitor) is in DMG trials.

## Receptor tyrosine kinase axis
PDGFRA mutation or amplification is enriched in H3.3 K27M DIPG (Paugh 2013, Mackay 2017).
Many RTK amplifications are only seen with copy-number data.

## H3 variant split
H3.1 and H3.3 K27M tumours differ in partner alterations, age and survival (Castel 2015, Mackay 2017).

## Measurement caveat
Panel and exome cohorts differ in which genes and copy-number events they report.
